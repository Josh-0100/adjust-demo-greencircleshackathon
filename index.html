How it works

STEP 1

Describe it plainly

"Our picker has back pain and can't lift all day." Adjust asks a question if it needs more.

STEP 2

Get sourced options

Practical adjustments from Acas, HSE and GOV.UK guidance, each showing where it came from and what it costs.

STEP 3

Apply for help

Step-by-step help with Access to Work, sick pay rules and a referral to WorkWell Bolton.

STEP 4

Keep a record

A one-page plan for you, and a plain-language copy for your employee to keep.
